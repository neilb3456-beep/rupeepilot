ABOUT THE PROJECT

Useful tools. Clear assumptions.

RupeePilot is an independent website concept offering simple calculators for common money and everyday questions in India. It is built as a static website using HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript.

What we aim to do

We aim to make common calculations easier to explore, explain assumptions in plain language and keep calculator inputs in the visitor's browser wherever possible.

What we do not do

RupeePilot is not a bank, lender, investment adviser, tax practitioner or government service. Results are estimates, not offers, guarantees or professional advice. Confirm important figures with the relevant institution or qualified professional.

Corrections and contact

For now, site feedback can be submitted through the public GitHub issue tracker. A dedicated monitored contact email should be added before monetisation applications.
