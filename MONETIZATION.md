# RupeePilot monetization

The private Sites preview serves no ads. Keep the publisher and slot blank there.

## AdSense activation on the public site

1. Use a public URL and an eligible publisher account. AdSense applicants must be 18 or older. If the site owner is younger, a parent or guardian can use their own account and receive payments.
2. Add the intended public URL to AdSense and use Google's verification instructions. Obtain a responsive display unit for the site.
3. Review the public site's privacy disclosures and the consent requirements that apply to its audience before activating ads.
4. In the public release, configure the publisher and slot constants in monetization.js from the AdSense dashboard. Publish the exact ads.txt record Google supplies if your account requests it. The placement is labeled and stays below the finance tools.
5. After AdSense reports the site ready, check the production URL and use AdSense reports for actual earnings.

At an illustrative page RPM of ₹100, ₹2,000 per week requires 20,000 monetized pageviews per week. RPM varies with traffic, geography and advertiser demand.
