Privacy policy

This starter policy describes the current static-site implementation. Review and update it before public launch to reflect every service you actually enable.

Calculator inputs

The calculators in this version run in your browser. The website code does not transmit the numbers you enter to a RupeePilot server. Your browser, hosting provider or optional third-party services may process technical information as described in their own policies.

Analytics and advertising

No analytics or advertising scripts are enabled in this starter release. If you later add Google Analytics, AdSense, affiliate widgets, embedded content or other third-party tools, update this policy to identify them, explain their purposes and link to their policies before enabling them.

Cookies

This version does not set first-party tracking cookies. Third-party services added later may use cookies or similar technologies. Disclose them accurately and provide controls where required by applicable law and provider policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site owner through the RupeePilot repository. Do not post personal or financial information in a public issue. A dedicated private privacy contact email should be added before third-party advertising or analytics are enabled.
