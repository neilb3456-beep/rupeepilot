    FIELD NOTES

    Reading
    the market.

    Short guides to market data, borrowing costs and setting savings targets.

    What does a close measure?

    A stock close is the closing price published for a trading session. A crypto daily close marks the end of the data provider’s daily interval in a market that keeps trading. Use the date and data source when comparing the two.

    Why compare prices on a base of 100?

    Putting both series at 100 gives them the same starting point. An asset moving from 100 to 110 rose 10% from its starting close. For a rupee-based investor, the return on a USD-quoted asset also depends on the exchange rate.

    What can a price chart tell you about risk?

    A chart shows how prices moved in the window you chose. A short window can miss larger drawdowns, and a closing price chart can miss swings within a session. Consider holding period, liquidity and concentration when researching an asset. SEBI’s guide to investment risk

    What does the EMI leave out?

    EMI combines principal and interest under a repayment schedule. Ask for the loan’s fees and review whether the rate can change. Two loans with similar monthly payments can have different total costs if their terms differ.

    How do I size an emergency fund?

    Start by listing expenses that would continue if your income stopped, such as housing, utilities, food and minimum debt payments. In the planner, vary the number of months to see how income stability and dependants affect your target.
