02 / STOCK DECODERVALUATION BASICS

Speak fluent
stock market.

Four common metrics. None is a magic buy signal on its own — context is everything.

P/E

Price-to-earnings

How much the market pays for each rupee of a company's annual earnings per share. Compare with similar businesses and the company's own history.

REMEMBER

A high P/E can reflect growth expectations — or an expensive share price. A low P/E can signal value — or serious trouble.

01Compare like with like

Use the same industry, period and accounting basis.

02Look for the whole picture

Pair valuation with growth, cash flow, debt and governance.

03Respect uncertainty

A good business can still be a bad investment at the wrong price.

THE INVESTOR'S CHECKLIST01 — 05
A RESEARCH-FIRST APPROACH

A ticker is
not a thesis.

A rising chart alone doesn't make a company a good investment. Build a view from business quality, earnings, valuation, balance-sheet strength and the risks that could break your thesis.

EDUCATIONAL FRAMEWORK · NOT A RATING

Five lenses.
One clearer view.

Use these lenses together. No single ratio or indicator can tell you whether a stock is worth owning.

01 / QUALITY

Can it compound?

ROCE, ROE, margins, competitive position and management execution.

02 / GROWTH

Is the engine real?

Revenue, earnings, cash flow and whether growth is repeatable.

03 / VALUE

What is priced in?

P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA and valuation against history and peers.

04 / BALANCE

Can it take a hit?

Debt, liquidity, interest cover, cash conversion and dilution.

05 / MOMENTUM

What is the market saying?

Trend, relative strength, volume, volatility and market regime.
