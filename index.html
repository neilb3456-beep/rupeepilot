UNDERSTAND THE NUMBERS

Quick answers. Clear assumptions.

How is EMI calculated?

For a reducing-balance loan, EMI is based on principal, monthly interest rate and the number of monthly payments. The estimate excludes lender fees, insurance and rate changes.

Can a SIP calculator predict returns?

No. A SIP calculator illustrates a hypothetical compounding scenario using the return assumption you enter. Market returns vary and can be negative; the estimate is not a promise.

How do I calculate GST?

To add GST, multiply the taxable amount by the selected rate and add the tax. To extract GST from an inclusive price, divide the inclusive amount by one plus the rate.

Are these bank or tax quotes?

No. Product terms, deposit compounding, tax treatment and applicable GST rates depend on the institution, transaction and current rules. Confirm with the relevant authority.
